## Gate A: redundant proxies

Layout: `cmd/proxy` containers on m2 and m3 (published on host port 3306, on the cluster overlay network), keepalived 2.3.4 (VRRP, unicast, VIP 192.168.104.250/24, `nopreempt`, check script = "local proxy reports a writer" through its status endpoint, interval 1 s, fall/rise 2). Clients ran on m1 (not a proxy VM). Four clients, 10 writes/s each, 40 writes/s total: through the VIP (new connection per write, one persistent connection) and with a client-side failover list `holder,other` (same two modes). The two options from ADR groundwork (VIP and client-side failover) were both evaluated; the proxies are independent processes with no shared state.

| Check | Observed |
| --- | --- |
| VIP election | One holder (m2, priority 150) 4 s after keepalived start; exactly one holder before every test and after every recovery. A client on m1 reached the cluster through the VIP (p50 177 us per `SELECT 1`). |
| Writer agreement (stable policy) | **Two independent proxies chose different writers**: m2 proxy `n2`, m3 proxy `n3` (first-reachable/lowest-latency choice per proxy; 196 and 194 writes, each only on its own writer). ## What this shows and what stays open

- Hard power loss of one node and of all nodes never triggers an automatic bootstrap by the manager (approval-gated design holds; "hands off" confirmed three times). Data written before each loss (sentinel rows) survived.
- Galera itself restores a full outage from `gvwstate.dat` when the last views agree. The product's guided recovery is needed only when this does not happen (mixed views, a node with an empty or diverged state). That case, and the guided recovery after a VM power loss, are **not covered**; E6 covered it for SIGKILL of the containers.
- Open and unexplained: the repeated exit-1 task failures of the third node after B2/B3 (run 1) and the "no Galera state" answer of the diagnosis after those failures. Needs a dedicated re-run with the slot logs captured (the capture is now in the script).
- The lab manager was restarted by the script after the power loss (it is a transient lab unit); with `deploy/stack.yaml` Swarm would restart it. Not tested here.
- Redundant proxies need a coordinated writer choice (above). VIP via keepalived works in lab (user-v2 network, unicast); multicast, cloud networks and ARP behaviour of other environments are untested. The `nopreempt` choice means the VIP stays on the peer after recovery.
- Scripted checks fixed late: the diagnosis helper phase `e8recover` exists for development only and was run once against a stuck lab.
